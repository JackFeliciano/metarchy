# Roadmap

## Done

- Thirteen services, fixed by name.
- Providers, members, listings and orders.
- Five refusals in the market.
- An order that records provider, service, amount and day.
- The cheapest listing and the services nobody covers.

## Next

- Payment. An order records an amount that a payment provider would settle.
- Delivery. The market records what a member took, and who offered it.
- Review. A member rates what they took, and the next member reads the rating.
- Coverage. A service nobody covers shows on the page as a gap, so a company can fill it.

## Out of scope

- Custody of money.
- Identity documents.
